# Sales-and-Incentive-Management-Dashboard-Power-BI- 

## Objective
Analyze sales performance against assigned targets and evaluate the impact of incentives, salary bands, and regions on achievement. This dashboard supports performance monitoring, incentive planning, and profitability analysis for sales leadership.

---

## Dataset Overview
- Sales revenue by salesperson  
- Assigned sales targets  
- Salary slabs  
- Commission percentages  
- Regional classification (East, West, North, South)  
- Total sales force: 43 representatives  

---

## Tools and Techniques
- Power BI Desktop  
- DAX for calculated measures  
- Interactive slicers (Region, Salary, Commission %)  
- KPI cards, tables, donut charts, bar charts, and gauges  

---

## Key Metrics
- Total Sales  
- Total Targets  
- Percentage of Achievement  
- Excess / Shortfall Amount  
- Number of Target Achievers  
- Total CTC  
- Profit  

---

## Key Insights
- Overall target achievement is **100.79%**, indicating targets were exceeded.
- **20 out of 43** salespersons achieved or exceeded their targets.
- Revenue contribution is skewed toward a small set of high performers.
- Certain regions consistently outperform others.
- Higher commission percentages show a positive correlation with achievement levels.

---

## Business Value
- Enables data-driven incentive and compensation planning.
- Helps identify top performers and underperformers.
- Supports regional strategy optimization.
- Improves transparency in sales performance tracking.
